\noindent Dear Editor,\\[1em]

\noindent Please find enclosed our manuscript entitled \textbf{``Transmission
dynamics of the 2026 Bundibugyo ebolavirus disease outbreak in the
Democratic Republic of the Congo: a compartmental model with an explicit
observation layer''} for consideration as an Original Research Article in
\emph{PLOS Computational Biology}.\\[1em]

The 2026 Bundibugyo ebolavirus disease (BDBV) outbreak in the Democratic
Republic of the Congo is, to our knowledge, the largest epidemic ever
caused by this species. Its confirmed-case curve conflates transmission
with detection, which has made earlier reading of its dynamics unreliable.
This work develops a path-mixed SEIHRD compartmental model coupled to an
explicit observation layer in which a logistic detection ramp maps
infections to confirmed cases. Calibrated on a verified 21-week UKHSA
series anchored at the outbreak declaration, the model separates
intrinsic transmissibility ($\Reff_0\approx1.73$) from the reporting
process, reconciles the early rise with slower intrinsic growth, and shows
that the present reproduction number has fallen below one while the
one-year projection band still spans a factor of 2.2. By treating the
detection ceiling as a quantity that carries no information from case
counts and must be fixed from independent anchors, the study makes a
methodological point that extends beyond this outbreak: transmission
estimates from poorly reported epidemics are only as credible as the
observation-layer assumptions behind them. We believe this combination of
an explicit, identifiable observation model and a policy-relevant
projection speaks directly to the readership of \emph{PLOS Computational
Biology}.\\[1em]

This manuscript is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not
under consideration by any other journal. The author is the sole author
and approves the submission. No individual-level or unpublished data were
used; all inputs are public surveillance series, so the study required no
research ethics committee approval. The author declares no competing
interests and received no specific funding for this work. The model code,
calibration pipeline, verified weekly series and all figure/table scripts
are deposited in a public repository archived with Zenodo
(DOI \texttt{10.5281/zenodo.XXXXXXX}; to be replaced with the real
deposited DOI).\\[1em]
